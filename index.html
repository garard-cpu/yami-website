    get the app

    put yami on your home screen.

    No app store, no download. Yami opens from an icon like any other app. Free while we test with USC students.

    iphone

    1. Open yami-usc.netlify.app/app in Safari.
    2. Tap Share, the square with the arrow.
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    4. Open Yami from your home screen and set up your profile there.

    android

    1. Open yami-usc.netlify.app/app in Chrome.
    2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
    3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
    4. Open Yami from your home screen and set up your profile there.

    what it does

    your plate, your order, your grocery run. sorted.

    📸

    snap a meal

    Take a pic of your plate. Yami reads every item, guesses the portion and gives you one easy add or swap.

    "Nice protein. Grab greens from the salad bar next time."
    🌯

    order smart

    Ten minutes before class? Yami picks the best option near you, from the dining halls to Starbucks and Chipotle.

    "Chipotle: chicken bowl, extra fajita veggies, skip the sour cream."
    🛒

    grocery cart

    Picks from Trader Joe's, Target and Ralphs that fit your goals and your budget.

    "Greek yogurt, berries, edamame. About $11 at Trader Joe's."
    the vibe

    made to feel good, not to grade you.

    10 seconds, tops

    Snap, glance, go. One tip, not a spreadsheet.

    no calorie math

    We talk energy, protein and plants. Never "good" or "bad" food.

    camera only when you snap

    No asking for your camera, location or mic the second you open it.

    real questions

    ok but...

    is this a calorie counter?

    No. Yami looks at what's on your plate and suggests one easy add or swap. If you ever want numbers, they're there, but they're not the point.

    is it actually from usc?

    It's built by USC students, but it's a student project. It's not official and not endorsed by the university.

    when can i use it?

    Right now. Add Yami to your home screen with the steps above. It's an early version we're testing with USC students, so tell us what's off. Join the list to get launch news and the top 5 meals list.

    do i need an account?

    Not yet. Your profile is saved on your phone, so open Yami from the home screen icon each time. If you delete the icon or clear Safari data, you'll set it up again.

    what happens to my email?

    We only use it to tell you when Yami is ready. No spam, and you can opt out anytime.
