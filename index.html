questions

frequently asked.

is yami free?

Yes. Yami is free for USC students.

is it only for usc?

For now, yes. Our menus and stores are the ones on and around USC's University Park campus.

do i have to count calories?

No. Calories stay off unless you turn them on. Yami focuses on your goal: protein, fiber, greens and one habit a day.

how accurate is it?

Campus and restaurant items use real menu nutrition, and groceries use nutrition labels. When Yami estimates something from a photo, it says so.

is this medical advice?

No. Yami gives general nutrition guidance. If you have a medical condition, follow your doctor's plan.
